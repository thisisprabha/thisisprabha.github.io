Privacy Policy for Time Left

Last Updated: August 09, 2026

Introduction

Prabhakaran ("we," "our," or "us") provides the Time Left app, a personal time tracking utility designed to help you track countdowns, count-up moments, habits, reminders, widgets, and leave planning. The app is provided for use as is.

This policy explains our collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

We respect your privacy. Time Left does not collect, sell, store, or transmit your personally identifiable information to our own external servers.

Local Data Storage

The data you enter into the app, including custom events, dates, habits, reminder settings, display preferences, widget settings, themes, and app settings, is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage technologies, including UserDefaults and App Groups.

  • Purpose: Local storage lets the app and widgets remember your events, habits, and settings.
  • Access: We do not have access to your personal events, habits, reminders, or schedule.

iCloud Sync

If you turn on iCloud Sync, Time Left may sync your events, habits, and settings using Apple's NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore. This sync is handled by Apple iCloud and is tied to your Apple ID.

  • Purpose: iCloud Sync helps keep your Time Left data available across your Apple devices.
  • Control: You can turn iCloud Sync on or off in the app settings.
  • Apple: iCloud data is subject to Apple's privacy policy and iCloud terms.

Notifications and Reminders

Time Left may request notification permission so it can send reminders for events and habits you create. Notifications are scheduled on your device. We do not receive your reminder content.

Calendar Access

If you choose to import events from Calendar, Time Left may request access to your calendar. Calendar access is used only to show and import events you select. We do not upload or sell your calendar data.

Log Data

In the case of an error in the app, anonymous crash or diagnostic information may be collected through standard Apple reporting tools. This may include information such as your device model, operating system version, and the time of the crash. This information is used solely to fix bugs and improve app stability.

Service Providers

Time Left does not use third-party analytics or advertising services that collect information used to identify you. iCloud Sync, Calendar, notifications, widgets, purchases, and crash reporting are handled through Apple system services where applicable.

Children's Privacy

Time Left is a general utility app safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective when posted on this page.
