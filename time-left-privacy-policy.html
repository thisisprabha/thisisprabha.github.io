Privacy Policy for Time Left

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Introduction

Prabhakaran provides Time Left, an app for countdowns, dates since a milestone, reminders, widgets, and optional Calendar import and iCloud sync.

This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

Information Collection and Use

Time Left does not require an account or send your dates to a server run by us. We do not sell your data or use third-party advertising or analytics in the app. Optional Apple services are described below.

Local Data Storage

Dates, habits, reminder choices, display preferences, and other settings are stored on your device. Time Left uses app storage and an App Group so its iPhone widgets can show the dates you choose.

  • Purpose: Storage lets the app and widgets remember and display your dates and settings.
  • Apple Watch: If you use the companion Watch app, relevant dates and settings are sent between your paired devices through Apple's WatchConnectivity service.
  • Access: We do not have access to your personal dates, reminders, or schedule.

iCloud Sync

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, Time Left copies supported dates, habits, and settings to your Apple iCloud account using NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore. Apple stores and syncs that information across your devices; we do not operate the iCloud service or have access to your synced data.

  • Control: You can turn sync off in Time Left Settings. Turning it off stops further syncing from the app; it does not necessarily remove data already stored in your iCloud account.
  • Apple: iCloud data is subject to Apple's privacy policy and iCloud terms.

Notifications and Reminders

Time Left may request notification permission so it can send reminders for events and habits you create. Notifications are scheduled on your device. We do not receive your reminder content.

Calendar Access

If you choose Import from Calendar, Time Left requests Calendar access and searches events on your device to suggest dates. An event is copied into Time Left only when you choose to import it. We do not upload your calendar contents to our servers.

In-app Purchases

Apple handles Time Left Pro purchases and restores through StoreKit. The app checks your purchase entitlement to unlock Pro features. We do not receive your payment card details.

Log Data

Apple may provide crash and diagnostic reports to developers according to your device's analytics-sharing settings. We use reports available to us to investigate app problems.

Service Providers

Time Left does not include third-party advertising or analytics services. iCloud, Calendar, notifications, widgets, Watch connectivity, purchases, and diagnostic reports use Apple services where applicable.

Children's Privacy

Time Left is a general utility app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective when posted on this page.
