Privacy

Privacy Policy

That’s My Font creates your font on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your handwriting or personal information.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

The short version: no account, no ads, no third-party analytics, and no handwriting uploads.

Information we collect

That’s My Font does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or your hand-drawn characters.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally so its features work and your progress is available when you return:

  • Hand-drawn character strokes
  • Character position, scale, and alignment adjustments
  • Drawing preferences such as tool, stroke width, and smoothing

This information is not sent to us or stored on our servers.

Font creation and export

OpenType font files are generated locally on your device. When you export a font, you choose where to save or share it through the iPadOS share sheet. The selected destination may be operated by Apple or another app you choose, and its own privacy policy may apply. We do not receive or retain the exported font.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. It does not sell or share personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Your drawings and settings remain on your device until you clear them in the app or remove the app. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data, subject to the normal behavior of your device and any backups you control.

Children’s privacy

That’s My Font does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Because no personal information is collected by the app, we do not maintain information about a user’s age.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date will be revised. Material changes will be reflected in the App Store privacy information where required.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to uxcurry@gmail.com.
