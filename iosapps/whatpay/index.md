# WhatPay: Bills & Subscriptions

> Track subscriptions, EMIs, trials, and recurring bills in one clean iPhone dashboard.

## Product

- **Platform:** iPhone
- **Requires:** iOS 18.0 or later
- **Category:** Finance
- **Current public version:** 1.3.3
- **Price:** Free for up to six tracked payments, with a one-time WhatPay Pro In-App Purchase for unlimited tracking
- **App Store:** [Download WhatPay](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6756521274)

## What WhatPay does

- Tracks subscriptions, EMIs, free trials, and recurring bills
- Shows monthly, yearly, and yet-to-pay totals
- Organizes payments by category
- Shows detailed payment breakdowns and due-soon insights
- Supports payment reminders and a Home Screen widget
- Synchronizes entries through the user's personal iCloud account

## Privacy

WhatPay does not require an account. Payment entries are stored on the user's device and synchronized through the user's personal iCloud account. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or ad tracking.
