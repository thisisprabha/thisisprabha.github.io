# WhatPay: Bills & Subscriptions

> Track subscriptions, bills, loans and free trials on iPhone, as blocks sized by what they cost.

## Product

- **Platform:** iPhone
- **Requires:** iOS 18.0 or later
- **Category:** Finance
- **Current version:** 1.3.4
- **Price:** Free to set up with every bill you pay. WhatPay Pro is a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase.
- **App Store:** [Download WhatPay](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6756521274)

## What WhatPay does

- Shows every subscription, bill and loan as a jelly block sized by its monthly cost
- Sets up from a catalog of local plans and prices, or imports from a screenshot read on the device
- One total per day, week, month or year, next bills first, and a calendar
- Loans and EMIs with payments left; free trials with a reminder the day before they end
- Keeps bills for a partner or parent apart
- Reminders before each payment, and iCloud sync across devices
- WhatPay Pro: adding bills after setup, insights, cheaper plan suggestions, cancel guides and Home Screen widgets

## Privacy

WhatPay needs no account and no bank login. Bills stay on the device and in the user's private iCloud. Screenshots are read on the device and never uploaded. To load local plans, prices and cancel guides, the app sends only the chosen country to its catalog server (hosted by Supabase). No analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking.
