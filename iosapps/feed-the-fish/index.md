# Feed the Fish

> A calm virtual aquarium for iPhone with no streaks, scores, badges, or daily jobs.

Drop food into a small pond and watch five fish gather, hesitate, and eat. The app does not punish absence or turn the pond into another task.

## Main Features

- Tap or drag to drop fish food
- Five fish, with an option to add more
- Fish grow and become familiar over time
- Water changes with the light outside
- Optional ambient sound
