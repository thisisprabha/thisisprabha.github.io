Help with your pond

A few useful answers, without making a small fish app sound complicated.

Email supportVisit the app page

How do I feed the fish?

Tap open water to drop a small pinch of food. You can also drag across the pond to scatter a trail of pellets.

Do the fish need me every day?

No. There is no streak to keep and the fish do not die when you are away. Open the pond when it suits you.

Why are there fewer pellets?

The food jar refills overnight. When it is empty, the pond tells you when the next refill is due.

Can I turn off notifications?

Yes. Notifications are optional. You can change that choice any time in iPhone Settings.

Is my pond backed up or shared?

Your pond stays on your device. The app does not use an account or send your pond to our servers.

Something not working? Restart the app first. If it still happens, email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description. A screenshot helps when the problem is visual.
