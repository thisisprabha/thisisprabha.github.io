A small pond for iPhone

Nothing to keep up with.

Five little fish live here. Feed them when you pass by, watch them squabble over lunch, and leave when you are ready.

Get Feed the Fish
The whole thing

Drop food. Watch what happens.

Tap the water for a small sprinkle, or drag your finger for a trail of pellets. The fish each have their own way of arriving, hesitating, and eating.

Five fish

They are not collectibles. They are simply the fish who already live in your pond.

Small changes

The water follows the light outside. The fish grow a little and learn that you are familiar.

Sound, if you want it

Food lands with small notes instead of a fanfare. It should feel like water, not a slot machine.

What this pond is not

Not another thing to keep up with.

The fish change a little over time, but they never turn your absence into a problem.

  • No streaks.Missing a day does not break anything.
  • No badges or scores.There is nothing to optimise or show off.
  • No daily jobs.Feed the fish when it sounds nice, not because a task says so.
  • No fish in trouble.They are fine while you are elsewhere.
Why we made it this way

Most days already arrive with something to complete. This pond is for the minute when you do not want to turn a break into another task.

Open it because you feel like seeing five fish—not because a red badge got there first.
