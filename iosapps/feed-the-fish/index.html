A small pond for iPhone

Nothing to keep up with.

Five little fish live here. Feed them when you pass by, watch them squabble over lunch, and leave when you are ready.

Get Feed the Fish
The whole thing

Drop food. Watch what happens.

Tap the water for a small sprinkle, or drag your finger for a trail of pellets. The fish each have their own way of arriving, hesitating, and eating.

Five fish

They are not collectibles. They are simply the fish who already live in your pond.

Small changes

The water follows the light outside. The fish grow a little and learn that you are familiar.

Sound, if you want it

Food lands with small notes instead of a fanfare. It should feel like water, not a slot machine.

On purpose

A pond is allowed to be just a pond.

Feed the Fish has no streaks, scores, badges, daily jobs, or pressure to return. The fish do not die when you are away. Come back because you want to see them.

  • Feed by tapping or dragging across the water.
  • Watch five distinct fish eat, swim, and grow.
  • Hear quiet feeding sounds and feel soft haptics.
  • Keep your pond on your device.
