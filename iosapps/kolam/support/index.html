Help with your kolam

Short answers to the questions people ask most.

Email supportVisit the app page

How do I solve a puzzle?

Draw one continuous line that loops around every dot. Each dot must sit inside its own turn of the line. When every dot is held and the line closes on itself, the kolam is finished.

I am stuck. Is there a hint?

Yes. Tap the lightbulb and the app traces part of the line for you. Use Undo to step back a stroke, or Clear to start the puzzle again.

How do I open the other collections?

Festival Shapes, Periya Pulli and the other collections open after you finish the Pulli / Sikku puzzles. Open Collections from the game screen to see what is left.

Where are my finished kolams?

Finished kolams are kept in the gallery inside the app. From there you can open any kolam again as a festival card.

How do I save or share a festival card?

Open the card and tap it to change its colours. The top-left button switches between square and portrait. The share button sends a short looping video of your kolam: choose Save Video to put it in Photos, or pick an app to send it. The first time you save, iOS asks for permission to add to Photos. You can change that later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud on both devices. Kolam keeps your progress in your own iCloud account, so it follows you to a new iPhone or your iPad. Without iCloud, progress stays on the device.

Can I turn off sound or vibration?

Kolam follows your iPhone's silent switch for sound. Haptics follow Settings > Sounds & Haptics.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no sign-up, and every puzzle works offline.

Something not working? Close and reopen the app first. If it still happens, email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, the puzzle number, and a short description. A screenshot helps when the problem is visual.
