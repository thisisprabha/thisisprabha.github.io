# Kolam

> A one-line drawing puzzle for iPhone based on kolam, the dot-and-line art of South India.

Draw one continuous line that loops around every dot. Finish the kolam, light a diya in its centre, and send it as a festival card. No timer, no score, no ads, no sign-up.

## Main Features

- 128 Pulli / Sikku puzzles, then Festival Shapes, Periya Pulli and lotus collections
- Hints, undo and clear
- Light a diya or place a flower on each finished kolam
- Festival cards in several colourways, saved to Photos or shared
- Works offline; progress syncs through the user's own iCloud account
- No ads, no account, no analytics
- First 20 kolams free; one-time Full Kolam purchase opens the rest (no subscription, Family Sharing)
