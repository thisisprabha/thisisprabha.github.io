# Time Left

> A private countdown and days-left widget app for iPhone.

Time Left puts birthdays, exams, trips, deadlines, and other important dates on the Lock Screen and Home Screen.

## Main Features

- Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets
- Count down to future dates or count up from past dates
- On-device storage with no account
- Optional iCloud Sync handled by Apple
- Local reminders
