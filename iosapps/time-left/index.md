# Time Left

> Countdowns, dates since a milestone, and widgets for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Time Left shows the time until birthdays, exams, trips, and deadlines, or the time since a milestone. Keep those dates on your iPhone Home Screen, Lock Screen, and paired Apple Watch.

## Main Features

- Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets
- Count down to future dates or count up from past dates
- Dates saved on your device with no account required
- Optional iCloud sync through Apple
- Local reminders
- Calendar import for dates you choose
