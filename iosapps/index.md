# iOS Apps by Prabhakaran

> Independent apps for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

## On the App Store

- [Time Left](https://thisisprabha.com/iosapps/time-left/): Count down to a date or see how long it’s been. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755923528)
- [WhatPay](https://thisisprabha.com/iosapps/whatpay/): See what your subscriptions, bills, and EMIs add up to. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6756521274)
- [Rail](https://thisisprabha.com/iosapps/rail/): Pick what to wear from your own closet and the weather. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6793017713)
- [Clickwork](https://thisisprabha.com/iosapps/clickwork/): Control Android TV or Google TV with tactile keys. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6830611585)
- [That’s My Font](https://thisisprabha.com/iosapps/thats-my-font/): Turn your handwriting into a font you can install. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755015714)
- [Leave Hack](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757073561): Find which days to take off to turn holidays into longer breaks.
