# iOS Apps by Prabhakaran

> Independent native SwiftUI apps for iPhone and iPad, designed and built by Prabhakaran Ravichandran.

## Apps

- [Time Left](https://thisisprabha.com/iosapps/time-left/index.md): Countdown and days-left widgets for birthdays, exams, trips, and deadlines. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755923528)
- [Feed the Fish](https://thisisprabha.com/iosapps/feed-the-fish/index.md): Preview of a calm virtual aquarium with no streaks, scores, or daily jobs.
- [Tamil Typo](https://thisisprabha.com/iosapps/tamil-typo/index.md): Preview of an iPad app for drawing Tamil characters and exporting an OpenType font.
- [That's My Font](https://thisisprabha.com/iosapps/thats-my-font/index.md): Turn your handwriting into an OpenType font on iPad. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755015714)
