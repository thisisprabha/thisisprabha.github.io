# Rail: Closet & Outfit Planner

> Rail looks in your closet every morning and tells you what to wear, from the clothes you own, the weather and where you're headed.

- Platform: iPhone
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6793017713
- Privacy Policy: https://thisisprabha.com/rail-privacy-policy.html
- Support: https://thisisprabha.com/rail-support.html

## Features

- One outfit ready every morning, picked for the forecast and the occasion (Office, Weekend, Date night, Party, Special occasion)
- Add clothes from the camera or Photos, even a mirror selfie; each garment is cut out on the device and sorted onto a shelf
- AI studio turns a messy photo into a clean studio photo; first 4 free, then one-time packs
- Tracks what's worn and what's in the wash
- Share today's look as a designed card or a selfie video

## Privacy

No account needed. Your closet stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud. Only pieces you choose for AI cleanup are sent for processing, after you allow it.
