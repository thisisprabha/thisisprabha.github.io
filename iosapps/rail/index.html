Questions, answered

Is Rail free?

Yes. Adding clothes and daily outfits are free, and your first 4 photo cleanups are free. More cleanups come in one-time packs, with no subscription.

Do I need to photograph clothes perfectly?

No. Clothes on a bed, a chair, a hanger or a mirror selfie all work. You can fix, merge or remove pieces before saving.

How does Rail know the weather?

With your permission, Rail uses your rough location about once a day to get the forecast from Apple Weather.

Where are my clothes stored?

On your iPhone, and synced through your own iCloud if it's on. We don't keep your closet on our servers.

Does it work on iPad?

Rail is made for iPhone and also runs on iPad.
