# Clickwork: Android TV Remote

> An iPhone remote for Android TV and Google TV with keys you can feel.

Clickwork turns an iPhone into a remote for Android TV and Google TV. Keys have travel, sound and haptics, in three finishes: Black, White and Retropad. The buttons change with what's on the TV, shortcuts learn the apps you use, and it can wake a TV that's in standby.

## Main Features

- Tactile keys with sound and haptics, in Black, White and Retropad finishes
- Finds the TV on Wi-Fi and reads the pairing code with the camera
- Play controls in YouTube and Netflix; channels, numbers, Guide and Input on live TV and HDMI
- Shortcuts that learn your apps, plus custom shortcuts for apps, links, keys and key sequences
- Type on the iPhone keyboard, or use voice on iOS 26 (transcribed on the iPhone)
- Wakes the TV over Wi-Fi on TVs that allow it
- Home Screen widgets, Control Center buttons and Siri
- 7 days free, then a one-time purchase; no subscription, no account

## Works With

Android TV and Google TV televisions (Sony, TCL, Philips, Hisense and others), Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer and NVIDIA Shield. Not Samsung, LG, Roku, Fire TV or Apple TV. The iPhone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi.
