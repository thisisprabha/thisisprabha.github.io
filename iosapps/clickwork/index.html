Press it. It presses back.

Most phone remotes are a grid of flat buttons. We spent our time on the part your thumb touches, and made each finish sound and feel like what it's made of.

A click for each finish

Knows what's on

In YouTube or Netflix, play and skip take the D-pad's place. On live TV or HDMI you get channels, numbers, Guide and Input.

Learns your apps

Watch the same app a couple of times and Clickwork offers a one-tap button for it.

Your own buttons

Make a shortcut for an app, a link, one key, or a run of presses like Input, down, OK. Long-press to change it.

Less typing

Type on your phone instead of picking letters with arrows. Or say it: "search lo-fi beats on YouTube", "HDMI 1", "pause".

Many Android TVs stop listening once they're off. Clickwork wakes them over Wi-Fi, screen and all, on TVs that allow it.
