Questions

Before you start drawing

What does Tamil Typo export?

Tamil Typo creates OpenType Font files with the .otf extension. You can export one Regular font or include Light and Bold versions.

Can I preview Tamil words before exporting?

Yes. Enter your own Tamil text to check the look, spacing, and rhythm of your characters before creating the OTF file.

Do I have to draw every character before exporting?

No. You can export after drawing at least one character, though a more complete set makes the font more useful in everyday Tamil text.

Where are my drawings saved?

Your progress is stored locally on your device so you can return to it later.
