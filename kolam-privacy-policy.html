Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your kolams belong to you, not to a server.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The short version: Kolam does not collect, upload, sell, or share your personal information. There is no account, no advertising, and no third-party analytics.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or information about how you use the app. Kolam does not connect to our servers.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your puzzle progress, your drawings in progress, the kolams you have finished, and your settings (such as card colours) on your device so they are there when you return.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Kolam keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it can sync between your devices. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see or access it. You can turn iCloud for the app off in iPhone Settings.

Photos

When you choose to save a festival card or kolam animation, iOS asks for permission to add images to your photo library. Kolam only adds the images you ask it to save. It does not read, browse, or upload your existing photos.

Sharing

When you share a festival card, the iOS share sheet sends it to the app or person you choose. We do not receive a copy.

Advertising and third parties

Kolam does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, crash reporting services, or tracking technologies. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

If the app offers an in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details or personal information from that purchase.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to your device backup settings. Progress kept in iCloud can be removed in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Children’s privacy

Kolam does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change too.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to uxcurry@gmail.com.
