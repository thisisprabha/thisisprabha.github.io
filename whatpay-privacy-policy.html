Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 (WhatPay 1.3.4)

In short: WhatPay has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your bills stay on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. The only thing the app sends to WhatPay's server is the country you pick, so it can show local plans, prices and cancel guides.

Who we are

WhatPay ("WhatPay", "we", "us") is an iPhone app made by Prabhakaran Ravichandran. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What you put into WhatPay

  • Bills: subscriptions, bills, loans and free trials you add, with their names, amounts, currency, billing cycle, payment dates, category, colour and reminder settings, and whether you marked them cancelled or paid off.
  • People: the names you give to people you track bills for, such as a partner or a parent.
  • Settings: your country, currency and app preferences.

WhatPay doesn't ask for your name, email, phone number, bank login or card details.

Where it's stored

  • On your iPhone, inside the app and its Home Screen widget (shared on the device through an App Group).
  • In your private iCloud, through Apple's CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage, so your devices stay in sync. This is your own iCloud storage. We can't read it.

WhatPay doesn't store your bills on any server of ours.

Importing from screenshots and files

If you import bills from screenshots, you choose the images with Apple's photo picker. WhatPay only sees the images you pick and doesn't get access to the rest of your photos. Text is read on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. Images are not uploaded and not kept after the import. CSV and text files you import are also read only on your iPhone.

Plans, prices and cancel guides

To suggest plans, show reference prices, find cheaper plans and show how to cancel a service, WhatPay downloads public reference data from its catalog server, hosted by Supabase. Each request contains only the country you chose (for example, US). No bills, amounts, names, device identifiers or Apple Account details are sent. As with any internet request, the server can see your IP address in short-lived technical logs. We don't use it to identify you. A copy of the cancel guides is kept on your iPhone so they work offline.

Reminders

Payment and trial reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a push server.

Purchases

WhatPay Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your payment details. The app asks Apple whether your Apple Account owns WhatPay Pro so it can unlock Pro features.

Third parties

  • Apple, for iCloud sync, in-app purchases and on-device text recognition.
  • Supabase, which hosts the public price catalog and cancel guides.

WhatPay has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any bill or person in the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your iPhone.
  • To remove the iCloud copy, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then iCloud, and manage WhatPay's data under your iCloud storage. You can also turn off iCloud for WhatPay there.

Children

WhatPay isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If WhatPay changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a translated version of this policy differs from this English version, this version applies.
