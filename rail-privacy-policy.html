Privacy Policy for Rail

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Introduction

Rail is a closet and outfit planner for iPhone, made by Prabhakaran. This policy explains what information Rail uses, where it goes and what choices you have. Rail does not require you to sign up, and it does not show ads or sell your data.

Information Collection and Use

Your closet, on your device

The photos you add, the cutouts Rail makes, your outfits, tags, what you've worn and what's in the wash are stored on your iPhone. Cutting clothes out of photos happens on your device. We do not upload your closet to a server run by us.

iCloud Sync

Rail can keep your closet in sync across your devices using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), in your own private iCloud database. We cannot see that data. Turning off iCloud for Rail in iOS Settings stops further syncing; it does not necessarily remove data already stored in your iCloud account.

Clean up photo (optional)

If you tap Clean up photo on a garment and allow it, Rail sends that one garment image to our server so it can be turned into a clean photo. Only the images you choose are sent. Nothing else in your closet leaves your device for this feature.

To clean up the photo, our server sends the garment image to an AI image model through OpenRouter (openrouter.ai), which passes it to the model provider. These providers process the image only to return the result. The finished clean photo is stored on our server so that a retried request does not charge you twice.

Before sending, Rail cuts the garment out of your photo. Try not to include faces or other people in photos you send for Clean up photo.

Anonymous Account and Purchases

To keep your cleanup balance, Rail creates an anonymous account identifier on your device. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. Cleanup packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Our server checks each purchase with Apple before adding photos to your balance. We do not receive your payment card details.

Location and Weather

If you allow it, Rail uses your approximate location about once a day to get the forecast from Apple Weather (WeatherKit) and pick clothes for it. Rail does not send your location to our server or keep a location history.

Camera, Photos and Selfies

Rail uses the camera and your photo library only when you add clothes or add a selfie to a share card. Selfies used for share cards are not stored by Rail and are never sent to our server. Faces are covered with an emoji by default.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning outfit notification, it is scheduled on your device. We do not receive its content.

Log Data

If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash and usage reports. Our server keeps basic request logs (such as time and error codes) to keep the service running and to prevent abuse.

Service Providers

Rail does not include third-party advertising or analytics. We use these services to run the app:

  • Apple: iCloud, App Store purchases, WeatherKit, notifications and diagnostics
  • Supabase: hosts our server and stores your anonymous cleanup balance and finished clean photos
  • OpenRouter and the AI model provider it routes to: clean up photos you choose to send

Deleting Your Data

Deleting Rail removes the data stored on your device. To delete data synced to iCloud, go to iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage. To delete your cleanup balance and any clean photos stored on our server, email us from the app's Settings or at the address below and we will remove them.

Children's Privacy

Rail is a general app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page, and the date above will change.
