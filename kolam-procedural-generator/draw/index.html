?How it works

Pulli are the dots; a sikku kolam is one continuous line that encloses every dot and weaves through every gap between neighbours. Fewer dots make a kolam you can actually copy onto your doorstep.

Your sketch is snapped to a small dot grid: a stroke that ends where it began fills with dots, any other stroke gets dots along it. The dots are kept as one connected shape, then a seeded search places mirrors on the lattice until a single closed loop covers it. “Another one” tries a new seed on the same dots.
