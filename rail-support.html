Getting Started

  1. Tap + and take or choose a photo of your clothes. A mirror selfie or clothes on a bed both work.
  2. Check the pieces Rail found. Remove extras, or tap "These are one piece" if a dress was split in two. Save.
  3. Open Today for an outfit picked from your closet and the weather. Swipe a piece to swap it, then tap "Wear this".

Common Issues

A garment was cut out badly

Use a plain background and good light, and keep the whole garment in the frame. On the review screen you can remove pieces or merge them before saving. You can also turn on AI cleanup for a clean studio photo.

Today isn't using the weather

Go to iOS Settings > Rail > Location and choose "While Using the App". Rail needs a few clothes in each shelf (tops, bottoms, shoes) to build an outfit.

A piece never shows up on Today

Worn pieces rest for a few days, and pieces marked as in the wash are left out until you mark them clean.

The morning notification isn't showing

Check iOS Settings > Notifications > Rail, and make sure the morning outfit is turned on in Rail's Settings.

My closet is missing on another device

Make sure both devices use the same Apple ID and iCloud is on for Rail. Syncing can take a few minutes.

Studio photos I bought aren't in my balance

Open Settings in Rail and wait a moment for the balance to refresh. Packs are one-time purchases and never expire. If photos still don't appear, email us with your app version and the date of purchase. Please don't send card details.

Privacy

Your closet stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud. Only garments you choose for AI cleanup are sent for processing. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Contact
