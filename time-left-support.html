Support for Time Left

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Time Left is a personal time tracking app for countdowns, count-ups, habits, reminders, widgets, and leave planning.

If something is not working right, this page covers the basics and tells you how to reach us.

Getting Started

Time Left is designed to stay simple. Most issues are fixed by checking app settings, notification permissions, or iCloud sync.

  1. Open the app and confirm your event or habit is saved.
  2. Check notification permissions if reminders are not firing.
  3. Check iCloud Sync if data is missing on another device.
  4. Force close and reopen the app if the UI looks stale.

Common Issues

Notifications are not showing

Make sure notifications are enabled for Time Left in iPhone Settings. If they are off, the app cannot send reminder alerts.

Data is missing on another device

Turn on iCloud Sync inside the app and make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID on both devices.

Widgets are not updating

Widgets may update with a delay depending on iOS. Opening the app once usually refreshes the widget state.

Calendar import is not working

Check that calendar access is allowed for the app. If permission was denied earlier, you may need to re-enable it in Settings.

Privacy

Time Left stores your data locally on your device and only uses Apple services like iCloud, notifications, and calendar access when you enable them.
