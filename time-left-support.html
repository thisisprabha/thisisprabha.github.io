Support for Time Left

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Time Left keeps upcoming dates and moments since they began in view, with iPhone and Apple Watch widgets.

If something is not working right, this page covers the basics and tells you how to reach us.

Getting Started

Choose Left for an upcoming date or Since for a date you want to remember. Tap + to add one. You can also import dates from Apple Calendar in Settings.

  1. Add a date in Left or Since, then choose how to display the remaining or elapsed time in Settings.
  2. To keep it visible, add a Time Left widget from your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen.
  3. If a date is missing on another device, check the optional iCloud sync setting on both devices.

Common Issues

Notifications are not showing

Turn on Event reminders in Time Left Settings, check that the individual date has a reminder, and allow notifications for Time Left in iPhone Settings.

Data is missing on another device

Turn on iCloud sync in Time Left Settings on each iPhone and use the same Apple Account. Sync may take a little time; open Time Left on both iPhones to let it refresh. Apple Watch receives dates from its paired iPhone.

Widgets are not updating

Open Time Left to confirm the date is still present. Widgets can refresh later than the app. If the widget is showing a different date, edit the widget and choose the date again.

Calendar import is not working

Open Settings → Import from Calendar. If access was denied earlier, allow Calendar access for Time Left in iPhone Settings, then return to the import screen. Search can find an event even if it is not suggested as a birthday or anniversary.

Pro purchase is not showing

Open Settings → Time Left Pro → Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us with the app version and the problem you see. Please do not send payment card details.

Privacy

Time Left stores your dates on your device. Optional iCloud sync stores supported data in your Apple iCloud account. Calendar access is used on-device to suggest dates for import.
